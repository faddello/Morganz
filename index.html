About Morganz

Morganz Integrated Solutions provides technical solutions tailored to real-world needs, with a focus on reliable systems that can be deployed quickly and managed easily.

We work across irrigation and smart agriculture applications to help customers reduce waste, simplify operations, and improve consistency.

What we deliver

  • Water-saving control: smarter schedules and practical automation.
  • Agricultural Projects Control: Fish farms, poultry and animal production systems to be monitored and controlled using our wide range of controllers equipped with various sensors.
  • Greenhouse control: smart control of greenhouse environment, irrigation and fertigation.
  • Adaptive control: Smart control system could be integrated to weather stations or satellite information resources to provide an adaptive control based on the collected data.
  • Simple operations: easy setup and management on mobile.
  • Supportability: systems designed to troubleshoot fast when needed.
  • Customization: business solutions and tailored controllers upon request.

Mission

Morganz’s mission is to help community use natural resources efficiently while maximizing performance by delivering smart, reliable, and easy-to-use control systems for irrigation and food systems, thanks for the modern AI, IoT and Sattelite informatin technologies. We reduce waste through intelligent scheduling, connected monitoring, and practical automation that supports healthier plants / animals while lowering effort and operating costs.

Vision

Morganz’s vision is a future where every landscape and farm is managed intelligently, achieving measurable water savings with better results. We aim to make smart agriculture and sustainable irrigation accessible at every scale, from home gardens to large commercial and agricultural sites.

Product

CYBERFLOW® WiFi Irrigation Controller

CYBERFLOW® enables end users to control irrigation via a mobile application. It is built for flexible scheduling, easy connectivity, and efficient water use that gives users peace of mind and convenience.

Technical features

  • Solenoid valve control (up to 16 valves per controller)
  • Motor pump control
  • Mist control support (optional depending on model)
  • Easy to use via mobile app (and Google Home app support as listed)
  • Designed for mobility and minimal wiring
  • Highly flexible scheduling algorithm
  • Sensors can be connected (model dependent)
  • Water/weather-proof enclosure
  • Waterproof sockets (no professional technician required)
  • Remote support access when convenient via WiFi/mobile hotspot
  • Easy online/offline access
  • Customizable upon request
  • 3-year warranty

Common use cases

  • Home gardens, lawns, and villas
  • Compounds and residential communities with multiple zones
  • Hotels, resorts, clubs, and commercial landscapes
  • Farms and greenhouses moving toward smart agriculture
  • Sites that want better control over water use and irrigation consistency
